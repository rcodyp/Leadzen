The cost of selling the old way

Global growth is lost to four preventable problems.

Manual research, invisible committees, decaying data and single-region playbooks quietly cap what your team can sell, and where they can sell it.

~72% of seller time
Reps research more than they sell

Most of a seller's week goes to non-selling work: compiling account information, formatting proposals, chasing the right contact. Every hour spent confirming a title is an hour not spent on a live conversation.

13 + 9 stakeholders
The deal stalls in a committee you can't see

A rep who maps one champion is mapping roughly a tenth of the room. Most of the buying committee never speaks to the seller directly until late in the cycle, if at all.

~30% annual decay
Contact data goes stale faster than you refresh it

A list built in January is measurably wrong by the following January. People change roles, companies restructure, and an accurate record quietly becomes a bounce or a missed decision-maker.

10.2 channels used
One region's playbook does not travel

A cadence tuned for a nine-to-five buyer sends outreach at 2 a.m. somewhere else. Approval paths, preferred channels and even the expected first touch shift by market. A single-region playbook is invisible waste.
