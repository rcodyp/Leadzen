Layout & section blueprint for a lead-gen site.

I studied the references you sent — Belkins, CIENCE, Callbox, MarketJoy, Lusha, Apollo, EasyLeadz — plus the two Dribbble shots. They share a remarkably consistent skeleton. Below is that skeleton drawn as wireframes: a full homepage flow you can react to, with alternate framings for the two sections that carry the most weight.

Everything here is intentionally gray and label-driven. We lock the framing first, then pour in the real niche, copy, imagery and brand color one section at a time.

What's shared across refs

Hero with floating proof cards → logo strip → a hard-numbers band → a bento/grid of services → numbered process → case-study metrics → testimonials → pricing → closing CTA. Trust is front-loaded everywhere.

From the Dribbble shots

The framing language: a centered hero floating metric + photo tiles, rounded "folder" cards, and a mixed-size bento grid for features. Black + one accent on warm white.

What's deliberately TBD

Exact niche, headlines, body copy, real logos/photos, and the final accent color. Amber shown is a placeholder. Pick layouts now; we fill meaning later.
