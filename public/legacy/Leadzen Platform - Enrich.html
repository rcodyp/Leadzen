What Enrich does

Every record stays current, automatically.

Waterfall verification

Every email and phone number is checked across multiple verification passes before it reaches a rep, cutting bounce and wrong-number rates.

This is the direct answer to the ~30% annual data-decay problem most B2B lists face unrefreshed.
Details +
Continuous background refresh

Enrichment runs on a continuous cycle, not a one-time export, so a list built in January is still accurate in June.

Role changes, company moves and re-orgs are picked up automatically, not discovered at send time.
Details +
Region-aware data handling

Contact data is handled in line with the jurisdiction it sits in — the same enrichment engine works whether the record is in Berlin, São Paulo or Bengaluru.

Firmographic context (funding, tech stack, headcount) attaches to every record, not just the ones a rep remembers to look up.
