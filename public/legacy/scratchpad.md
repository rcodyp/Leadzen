# Fresh Deck — Ministry of Tribal Affairs (Degree 360)

## Brief
- Refine, don't reinvent: keep WARMTH of the old deck, but cleaner / more premium / editorial.
- Mood: cultural & heritage-rich · bold & confident · warm & human · premium/editorial.
- Audience: Ministry / Secretary-level formal pitch. 1 hour. ~150 slides (built in batches).
- Imagery: full-bleed cinematic photography, ONE per slide (placeholders for now).
- Language: English. Type pairing is Devanagari-aware (Indian character) but set in English.
- Keep the "liveliness" energy of every slide — but achieve it with typographic scale,
  warm imagery and gold accents, NOT dense translucent boxes + sprockets like the old deck.

## What the OLD deck looked like (to move away from)
Saturated marigold+oxblood, full-bleed photo collages, heavy condensed caps grotesque +
Cormorant serif, dark translucent text boxes crammed with copy, gold film-strip sprockets,
low whitespace. Loud, maximalist, advertisement energy.

## FRESH SYSTEM
Type:
  - Display/Headline: 'Rozha One' (high-contrast Latin+Devanagari editorial serif)
  - Body/UI: 'Mukta' (humanist Indian sans, ITF)
  - Kicker / source / data labels: 'IBM Plex Mono'
Palette (warm, refined):
  - paper  #f3ead9 (warm off-white)
  - ink    #211a12 (warm near-black)
  - gold   oklch(.74 .13 72)  ~#d49a31  (refined marigold — primary accent)
  - clay   oklch(.58 .13 45)  ~#b5562f  (terracotta)
  - maroon oklch(.40 .10 30)  ~#6c2a20  (deep oxblood — depth fields)
Motif: thin gold rule + letterspaced mono kicker + slim section counter (refined nod to old "1/4").
Photos: full-bleed, warm cinematic, anchored bottom-left protection gradient for text.

## Layout archetypes (built in the starter set)
1. Cover — full-bleed photo, scrim, gov kicker, big serif title, campaign line, credit
2. Section divider (PART 1) — deep maroon field, ghost numeral, gold section title
3. Opening narrative — portrait photo + ink panel of serif statements
4. Problem — editorial split, paper field
5. Strategic reframe — big-type idea on ink
6. Pillar — number + objective/message/tone grid over toned image
7. Data — large gold numeral, supporting stats, mono source
8. Quote — large serif quote, attribution
9. Channels — clean column grid
10. Closing — statement + credit

## Title sequence style
Topic noun-phrases, set in title case serif, with a small mono kicker above
(e.g. kicker "THE STRATEGIC REFRAME" / title "From beneficiaries to definers.").
Counters like "01 / 04" appear on pillar/series slides as a refined motif.
